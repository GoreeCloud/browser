# GoreeCloud Browser Identity Brief

## Status

**Resolved — canonical Browser identity established.**

The previous artwork-pending design cycle is historical context only. Current branding authority is `GoreeCloud/branding-assets`, where the Browser identity is the blue compass mark.

Verified authority revision: `a831479976fd3f82dc31cf9b7785145a757e7e28`.

## Current identity objectives

The Browser identity must remain:

- immediately recognizable at small launcher and chrome sizes;
- visually coherent with GoreeCloud and Glaze UI;
- distinct from third-party browser brands;
- usable in full-color and monochrome contexts;
- resilient under high contrast, reduced transparency, themed-icon, and constrained presentation modes;
- traceable to canonical branding sources rather than consumer-repository invention.

## Consumer rule

Browser repository artwork is derivative. Any future identity change must be made and approved in `GoreeCloud/branding-assets` first, then synchronized into Browser platform derivatives with provenance and rendered validation.

## Historical note

Earlier Browser identity explorations, including the aqua Tabmark/window concept and earlier rejected concepts, are superseded design history. They are not current branding authority and must not be presented as approved current identity.

## Production boundary

Canonical artwork establishes identity only. Linux launcher rendering, Android launcher rendering, packaging, accessibility, representative-device review, release, and production acceptance remain separately governed.
