The current GoreeCloud Browser identity is the blue compass mark defined by the canonical branding repository.

Files retained in this repository under `assets/branding/` are synchronized consumer derivatives required for compilation, packaging, launcher integration, and offline runtime use. They do not become independent branding authority.

Consumer derivatives must remain traceable to the canonical GoreeCloud branding source and must not silently diverge. Stale Tabmark/window artwork, generic browser symbols, third-party browser branding, and independently invented Browser marks are not current identity.

Branding establishes product identity only. It does not establish implementation maturity, Privacy Shield acceptance, Wardveil protection, release status, deployment state, or production acceptance.
